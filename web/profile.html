How to read it. xs and ys are the two dense encoder matmuls, scores is the causally-masked Gram matrix, rope is the rotary phase application, nzscan indexes the non-zero entries so the score loop can skip zeros, and count is the sparsity measurement itself.

What it is used for. The two 4-way unrolled loops in bdh.js were added on a hypothesis and first measured at no effect, on a machine that was running two training jobs at the time. Re-measured on a quiet machine they are worth roughly 1.9× on xs and 2.0× on ys. A benchmark taken on a busy machine is not a weak result, it is not a result.
